Workflow ID: PAYINFO_CHG_LUM | Company: Lumina Retail NV (BE, PC 311) | Status: active since 2026-03-01
Trigger: change of Payment Information (bank account) via the ESS portal MyPayslip, or manual entry by an SD Worx payroll consultant.
Validation: IBAN check digits and account holder name must match the employee record (hard stop).
Approval: a bank account change is released to payroll only after approval by a second SD Worx payroll consultant (four-eyes). Rule active since 2025-06-02.
Call-back: field 'Call-back done (Y/N)' on the change form, optional.
Audit: every change is logged with channel (ESS / MANUAL), user and timestamp.
